## Keeping the memory of deceased loved ones 'alive' - is this ethical? 

According to [an article in Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/meta-granted-patent-for-ai-llm-bot-dead-paused-accounts-2026-2), Meta have filed for a patent for an LLM to continue to simulate a person's social media activty, for example after they have died. 

I wonder how healthy and / or ethical that really is.
