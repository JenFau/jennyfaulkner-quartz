Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that creates new, original content—like text, images, music, or code—by learning patterns from vast datasets and then generating novel outputs that mimic those learned structures, essentially producing something unique from what it's studied.

Unlike traditional AI that analyses or classifies data in order to make predictions, Generative AI produces entirely new material.

It combines both elements of unsupervised, supervised and reinforcement learning with deep learning architecture. LLMs are typically Generative AI.

## LLM models

Current LLM AI models include ChatGPT, Gemini, etc.  Open source models also exist, such as Llama, Mistral, Gemma etc

## Generative AI models

- GANS
- Transformers
- Variational autoencoders (VAE)
- Neural radial fields (NeRF)
- Flow-based generative models
- Energy-based models (EBMs)
