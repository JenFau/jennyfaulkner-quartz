Whilst large language models (LLMs) have broad general knowledge, small language models (SLMs) have specialised knowledge, such as domain-specific knowledge.  An example of a SLM might be a healthcare SLM, trained specifically on medical data sets. Source: https://www.redhat.com/en/topics/ai/llm-vs-slm

SLMs are less heavy on computational resources for both training and deployment. In some cases, therefore, enterprises might benefit from choosing SLMs over LLMs. See https://www.techradar.com/pro/small-models-big-wins-four-reasons-enterprises-are-choosing-slms-over-llms

LLMs are frequently fine tuned using [instruction tuning](https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/instruction-tuning).
